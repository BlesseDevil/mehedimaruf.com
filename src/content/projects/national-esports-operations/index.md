From 2022 to 2023 I worked with Tesseract Esports, an esports production company based in Hyderabad, India, on the events it delivered in Bangladesh. The projects included work for Grameenphone and Tencent, with a combined project value of approximately BDT 50,000,000.

## What I did

- **League operations.** Ran operations for leagues with 1,000+ registered teams, which is 5,000 to 7,000 players: registration, scheduling, match administration, rule enforcement and results.
- **Team coordination.** Worked inside a league operations team of 60+ people and an official crew of 90+, keeping match days moving across many simultaneous games.
- **Client relations.** Was a point of contact between organisers, sponsors and broadcast partners, so that what was promised to a client matched what the operations team could deliver.

## Why it mattered

At this scale an esports league is a logistics problem before it is a broadcast. Thousands of players have to be in the right match at the right time, disputes have to be settled quickly and consistently, and the broadcast only works if the bracket underneath it is running on schedule.

## Related

Alongside the events I administered the official online communities of Arena of Valor Bangladesh (12,000+ members) and Honor of Kings Bangladesh (18,000+ members), handling moderation, member support and engagement.
