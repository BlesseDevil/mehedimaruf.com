The Grameenphone Community Cup was a community esports tournament in Bangladesh in 2023. I directed it end to end as Project Manager at Infinity Incorporation BD, on a budget of BDT 600,000.

## What I did

- **Sole director.** Owned the plan, the schedule and the budget from the first brief to the final broadcast.
- **Operations.** Coordinated the operations team: registration, brackets, match administration and results.
- **Broadcast.** Coordinated the broadcasting team, so that production and the tournament schedule stayed in step.
- **Problem solving on the day.** Live events run on fixed air times. When something broke, it had to be fixed inside the schedule, not after it.

## What it shows

One person holding the budget, the operations and the broadcast means every trade-off is made in one place. The event was delivered on its budget and on its schedule.
